← LearnX Privacy Policy Last updated: 12 October 2026 LearnX ("we", "us") is a learning website with an AI chat assistant, available at learnxtech.vercel.app. This page explains what information we use and why. 1. Information we collect Account details: your name and email address, and your profile picture if you sign in with Google. If you sign up with email, your password is stored only as a secure hash, never as plain text. Your content and activity: AI chats, notes, flashcards, bookmarks, course progress, quiz scores, XP, streak and app settings. 2. Google user data and Google Drive If you sign in with Google, LearnX may ask your permission to use the Google Drive "application data" folder (scope: drive.appdata). This is a hidden folder that only LearnX can open. We use it to save your LearnX data (chats, notes, progress, settings) in your own Google Drive, so it stays with you and syncs between your devices. We cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive. We do not sell your Google user data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with others except as needed to run the features you use. No human reads your Google user data unless you ask us to, or it is required for security or by law. LearnX's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. 3. AI features When you send a message to the AI assistant, the text (and any photo or file you attach) is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply. Please do not share passwords or highly sensitive personal information in chats. 4. Where your data is stored On your device (in your browser/app storage). In your Google Drive app-data folder, if you sign in with Google and allow it. On our servers: only basic account information (name, email, password hash, account status) and shared content such as courses and quizzes. 5. Your choices You can remove LearnX's access to your Drive at any time in your Google Account under Security → Your connections to third-party apps and services. You can delete your chats and notes inside the app, or ask us to delete your account by emailing the address below. 6. Security We use HTTPS and store passwords as hashes. No online service is 100% secure, so please keep your account safe. 7. Changes We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do. Contact: axelraai90@gmail.com
